# Signal Stadium interactive prototype

Open the [arena](signal-stadium-wall.html), [operator console](signal-stadium-operator.html), [player phone flow](signal-stadium-player.html), and [event report](signal-stadium-metrics.html) in tabs from the **same local origin**. For example, from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 65110 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory prototypes
```

Then visit `http://127.0.0.1:65110/signal-stadium-wall.html` and use the quiet **Operator** link. In **Event → Game/Rules**, set the player range, start mode, join window, round duration, and judge policy. Open [Event → Leads](signal-stadium-operator.html#leads) to turn the postgame follow-up form on or off for that event and choose the build-guide and expert CTAs. The **Fields** view lets an admin add, remove, rename, reorder, and mark up to eight fields required. The default fields are Name, Work email, and Company; there is no default Use case field. At least one email or phone field is needed while follow-up requests are enabled. Click **Save settings** and look for the revision confirmation. In **Prompts**, add or edit a challenge (maximum 55 characters) and queue it. In **Run**, set the ready count, start a heat, simulate a judge candidate, or award one or several winners. **No valid match** is also available. Prompt and timing changes saved during a heat take effect on the next heat; lead settings take effect when saved.

Use **Metrics** in the console navigation to open the event report; [sample activity](signal-stadium-metrics.html?sample=1#overview) is available for design review and is kept separate from real demo records. Its event selector, date presets, and custom dates filter landing sessions, unique joiners, engagement actions, heat and prompt results, CTA conversion, and the lead register together. The lead roster's **Details** action shows every saved form answer in a paged view. The report can export filtered event-log and lead CSVs; lead columns use stable field IDs, with the saved labels and types in each row's field snapshot. Playing requires no contact details. After a result, the phone shows the Twilio product explainer; a visitor can then request a build guide or an expert conversation. Submitting that form saves a lead for the active event with its time, request type, anonymous participant ID, heat, configured fields, and separate email-updates choice. Turning capture off removes those follow-up forms and stops new requests without deleting prior records. Analytics events contain no contact fields. The proposed spectator category vote and live messaging channels are described in [the spectator and operator flow](../docs/concepts/spectator-and-operator-flow.md); they are not implemented in this prototype.

This is a **visual prototype**. It uses illustrated camera tiles, a decorative QR graphic with a clickable player link, simulated judging, and browser `localStorage`. The player can optionally enable a **local-only** camera preview. Contact details entered in the demo are saved only in that browser and are never sent to Twilio, email, or a CRM. Browser-local settings, metrics, and leads persist after reload for that origin, but they are not shared between devices and are not suitable for live expo collection. Use test contact details in the prototype. A live build needs Twilio Video, server-owned timing and prompt secrecy, a validated vision referee, a shared database, authenticated operators, event-time-zone reporting, and real analytics/lead delivery.
